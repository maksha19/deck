This document contains a refined draft email to pitch the **Digital Pre-Engagement Platform** to the Ministry, incorporating personal observations of MPS wait times and specific data points on AI-driven impact.

**Subject:** Reimagining MPS: A Data-Driven Proposal for Digital Pre-Engagement

**Dear [Minister/Permanent Secretary/Director Name],**

In a recent Meet-the-People session, I observed firsthand the significant time citizens spend waiting—often up to an hour—for what is typically a brief 5-10 minute interaction. This experience highlighted the urgent need to bridge the gap between citizen expectations and operational capacity.

We are proposing the **Digital Pre-Engagement Platform**—a solution that uses AI to transform MPS from a reactive intake process into a proactive resolution model.

### AI Involvement: Beyond Digital Forms
Our platform utilizes AI as a powerful force multiplier for officers:
*   **Structured Synthesis:** AI processes asynchonous citizen submissions (video/audio/text) into concise, structured briefs, highlighting key pain points instantly.
*   **Automated Agency Flagging:** Key issues are identified by the system to automatically alert relevant agencies, allowing for pre-coordination 24-48 hours before the physical meeting.
*   **Inclusive Communication:** Residents can speak in their preferred language, while AI ensures officers receive accurate, synthesized summaries in the working language of the civil service.

### Why This Matters (Target Impact):
Our model is designed to achieve measurable improvements in service delivery:
*   **20–30% Reduction in Wait Times:** By shifting fact-finding to the pre-engagement phase.
*   **25% Decrease in Repeat Visits:** Ensuring more cases are "right first time" due to advanced officer preparation.
*   **100% Preparedness:** Every officer enters the room with the full context of the case, maximizing the value of the human interaction.
*   **Citizen Satisfaction:** A projected 15-20% increase in the satisfaction index through reduced friction and better listening.

We have developed a functional prototype and are eager to discuss a pilot program to demonstrate these outcomes in a live environment.
